What is here is "Monster_Creatures_Fantasy(Version 1.3).zip", which the store page
offers as a SEPARATE add-on download, not the pack itself. It is 26 KB and holds 13
files: one Attack3 sheet and one projectile sheet per creature, and nothing else.

  Flying eye/Attack3.png    900x150   6 frames @ 150x150
  Goblin/Attack3.png       1800x150  12 frames
  Mushroom/Attack3.png     1650x150  11 frames
  Skeleton/Attack3.png      900x150   6 frames
  ...plus Sword/Bomb/projectile sprites

No idle, walk, run, hurt or death in any of them, so no creature here can drive a
player rig. The file still needed is the main download, "Monsters_Creatures_Fantasy.zip"
(~66 KB), from the same store page.

Contents of that base pack (per the store page):
  Skeleton     idle 4, walk 4, attack 8, shield 4, take-hit 4, death 4
  Mushroom     idle 4, run 8,  attack 8,           take-hit 4, death 4
  Goblin       idle 4, run 8,  attack 8,           take-hit 4, death 4
  Flying Eye   flight 8,       attack 8,           take-hit 4, death 4

Why this pack: every creature carries the attack / hurt / death animations that both
the 0x72 knight and the ansimuz Gothic hero lack, and the four of them read as a
devolution ladder (feral humanoid -> organic -> stripped to bone).

The Flying Eye was also going to supply the Wings evolved trait. That no longer blocks
on this pack — wings now come from the demon already sitting in
02_ansimuz_gothicvania_collection, cut by tools/build_wings.py, which has the advantage
of being the same artist as the player's Gothic hero. See ART_RESOURCES.md.
